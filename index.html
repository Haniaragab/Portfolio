What I do

I direct and edit, and I'm happiest when I can follow a story from the first idea to the final cut.

Language has never been a barrier for me. I've directed and edited films in Arabic and Russian as well as English, including Still Here, which reached the top 10 at the third round of selection for Le Court Film Festival.

How I work

I strive for academic success and for perfection in everything I make, and I don't consider a project finished until every detail is right.

I'm quiet by nature, but I tend to end up leading, and I work just as well on my own as with a crew. If I don't know how to do something yet, I take it on anyway and learn it properly.
